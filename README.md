
Introduction

This report presents a detailed analysis of the sales data of Monstra Group for the year 2023. The analysis aims to provide actionable insights to support strategic decision-making for the upcoming year. The dataset includes Product ID, Sale Date, Sales Rep, Region, Sales Amount, Quantity Sold, Product Category, Unit Cost, Unit Price, Customer Type, Discount, Payment Method, and Sales Channel. The focus is on key performance indicators including sales by sales representative, sales by product category, sales by region, sales by customer type, monthly sales trends, sales by payment method, and sales by sales channel.

 Observations

1. David performed best for the year with a grossing revenue of £1,141,737.36

2. Charlie Performed least for the year with a grossing revenue of £860,811.48

3. January is the best month in the year for revenue generation with a grossing of £495,420.37

5. The North region performed best out of all regions with a revenue generation of £1,369,612.51

6. Clothing is the best performing product with the highest grossing revenue of £1,313,474.36

7. New Customers purchased more items than returning customers

8. Customers purchased more in stores than online

9. Customers purchased more with Credit Cards than cash and bank transfers

 Insight

1. Charlie performed least for the year. I do recommend to the stakeholders that Jan should be sent over for some training

2. We could replicate measures implemented in January for months with low sales turn out

3. The North region performed the least for the year. I do recommend to the stakeholders that the management of the North which is the best performing region to be transferred to the South in other to boost sales in the South.

4. I recommend that the stakeholders inform all inventory staff to keep an eye on the clothing stock level in the coming year such that the category does not get low in stock

5. Old customers should be contacted with promotional offers to encourage them to purchase more items
